On the beat

These are the eight shapes the crew can put over a cut, drawn here by the same code that draws them onto the film — not pictures of them. Tap one to change how it arrives.

How it arrives

A graphic that fades up reads as a caption. One that overshoots and settles reads as something that landed. It is the difference between a watermark and an edit.

How busy

Layered against the downbeats, up to three at once. Nothing sits still for long: the reference reels never leave a graphic on screen for more than about a second.

Over a cut

A rehearsal at the cadence a hypercut runs at, with the graphics landing where the crew would land them.
