Cut it like one of these

Each of these is a real reel, read shot by shot: where every cut falls across its whole runtime, and how bright and saturated it was at each one. Choosing one cuts your pictures to its timing — not to an average of its speed. None of the footage is here; a template is a list of numbers.

Add a reel of your own

Hand it a reel and it will watch it and write down the timing. The reel itself is measured and then deleted — what is kept is the list of cut positions, which is what you cut to. It needs a few seconds of footage and at least eight cuts in it to be worth copying.

Your choice is remembered for the next film.
